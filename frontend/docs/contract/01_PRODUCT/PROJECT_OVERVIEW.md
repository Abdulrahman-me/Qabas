# Project overview

**Purpose:** explain the product and audience. Technical contracts, implementation steps and review status belong to their dedicated documents.

Qabas (قبس) helps people learn Islam step by step, from their first question to real understanding. The name means a small flame carried to provide light and warmth. The experience should feel calm, welcoming, respectful, discreet and grounded in attributable sources.

The initial audiences are people curious about Islam (Explorers) and new Muslims. Arabic and English are the initial languages. Explorers begin with Unit 0, a short foundation of concrete questions (how we know things, whether there is a Creator, why guidance and revelation would matter) before Islamic claims are presented for evaluation; new Muslims begin with Unit 1. After Unit 0 both follow one shared curriculum: each lesson has an Explorer and a New Muslim variant of the same canonical lesson, authored in Arabic and localized into English. Content adapts its wording and explanations without generating a separate published lesson for each person. Nobody is asked their religion; onboarding asks what the learner is curious about and explains how the journey leads there.

Learning follows a visual journey (the Roadmap: what to learn next) of units and lessons of about six to ten minutes, each answering one central question completely enough to leave a useful understanding, never a dictionary-card definition. Discover offers lessons that stand on their own (what can I explore now?); they are the same lessons, never separate versions. Lessons combine clear teaching, authentic stories, interactive activities and source explanations, following a lesson-specific learning arc rather than a fixed template. Learners practise, meet terms through an accessible personal glossary, return for spaced reviews, and apply what they learn through small real-life challenges.

Raqeeb (رقيب) is a source-based assistant for questions, explanation and verification, with abstention or specialist referral when appropriate. Community features provide encouraging progress, streaks, quests, achievements, leagues and friendly challenges. Reviewer tools support controlled content production and publication.

The Flutter proof of concept establishes the intended design and interaction language. Its one complete Salah lesson is a reference example within a much larger product. Production content can contain many units, topics and lesson structures.

The interface character is a replaceable, optional Flutter companion. It is distinct from Raqeeb's assistant identity and from historical religious figures.

Taglines: **تعلّم الإسلام خطوة بخطوة، من أول سؤال إلى فهم متين.** / **Learn Islam step by step, from your first question to real understanding.**
